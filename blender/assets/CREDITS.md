## International Space Station (ISS) Model
- **Source**: Procedural CAD / 3D reconstruction based on official NASA 3D Resources and architectural design blueprints from NASA Johnson Space Center & NASA Ames Research Center.
- **Reference URLs**:
  - NASA 3D Resources: https://nasa3d.arc.nasa.gov / https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources
  - NASA ISS Reference Guide & Fact Sheets: https://www.nasa.gov/international-space-station/
- **License**: Public Domain / CC0 (US Government Works / NASA Open Data).
- **Architecture**:
  - Integrated Truss Structure (ITS) spanning S0 through S6/P6 with Solar Alpha Rotary Joints (SARJ)
  - 8 Photovoltaic Solar Array Wings (SAW) on articulated gimbals
  - Active Thermal Control System (ATCS) accordion radiator panels
  - Pressurized Module Cluster: Zvezda Service Module, Zarya (FGB), Destiny US Laboratory, Harmony (Node 2), Unity (Node 1), Columbus (ESA), Kibo (JAXA) with Exposed Facility porch, Quest Airlock
  - Earth-facing 7-window Cupola observation dome (Nadir)
  - International Docking Adapter (IDA-2 / PMA-2) with laser docking targets and strobe beacons
  - Canadarm2 (SSRMS) mobile manipulator robotic arm
